Before We Kick Things Off…

Is DriesTech the right fit for your project? 

For a partnership to succeed, we need to be the right team for the job, and you need absolute confidence in handing it over, when needed. Before any formal agreement is signed, 3 things

01

Codebase Access & Risk Assessment

We have to properly assess the technical health of your system

If you are bringing us an existing codebase, DriesTech requires full access to the source up front under a strict NDA.

If full access is not possible up front, we include a clause in our engagement that allows either party to walk away. Some codebases turn out to be unmaintainable whether due to severe technical debt or indefensible security risks. Walking blindly into that situation is a liability for both of us.

02

Two-Question Audit

Our initial development assessment is designed to answer 2 critical questions

Scalability: Can your current architecture support continued development and future features?

Continuity: Can DriesTech successfully take over the project from your previous team?

By that, reconstruct the original developer’s design intent, audit code readability, check architectural coherence, and ensure the foundation can support your upcoming product roadmap.

03

Zero Vendor Lock-In

We firmly believe in earning your partnership, not forcing it

DriesTech deliberately avoids proprietary dependencies, hidden code, or opaque implementations. The software DriesTech engineers is entirely yours, giving you the full freedom to maintain, extend, or transfer it to another provider whenever it suits your business.

If this transparent approach aligns with your values, and we have the availability to deliver exceptional work → WE ARE READY TO GET GOING.
