Live online

Project overview

Qaabil is a live learning platform for Cambridge IGCSE and A-Level students. Its public site lets visitors browse courses by qualification and see what each course covers. Signed-in learners can watch lessons, contribute videos, take quizzes, and track their progress.

Students, moderators, quiz builders, and administrators each have a panel built around the work they do.

Courses organised by learning objective

Content follows a hierarchy from qualification and course through sections, chapters, and learning objectives. Videos and quizzes attach to an objective so students can find material for the specific topic they are studying.

Administrators can import course structures from a spreadsheet instead of creating each section, chapter, and objective individually.

Video contributions and moderation

Students can record a video explaining a learning objective and submit it for review. Moderators can approve the video or send feedback. An approved contribution can unlock course access under Qaabil’s access rules.

Approved videos sit alongside the objective they explain. Learners can filter contributions by language and focus type.

Timed quizzes and useful feedback

Learning objectives have quizzes and chapters have tests. Questions have individual countdowns and lock when time expires. The question formats include single choice, searchable dropdowns, multiple selection, and answers with written reasoning.

After submission, the learner sees their score and a question-by-question breakdown that compares their answers with the correct ones. They can retake the quiz to practise.

Progress, badges, and reports

Objective statuses show whether a learner has started, needs more practice, or has reached mastery. Those statuses roll up into course progress. Students can download progress reports and log their own marks on past papers.

Creator and reviewer badges recognise contributions. The dashboard shows progress towards the next badge, and earned badges come with downloadable certificates.

Tools for teachers and administrators

Teachers can filter the question bank by syllabus topic, difficulty, marks, and question type, then assemble a printable test paper or marking scheme. Running totals update as they add questions.

Administrators manage questions, past papers, users, roles, badge settings, and public site content from the admin panel.

Live deployment

Qaabil is live online. The dashboard preview shows badge progress with account details omitted.
